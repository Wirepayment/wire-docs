---
title: Whose name to register in
description: How to register a business, its projects and its receiving account under the right owner.
---

On Wire a business is registered by its **real owner**, and income goes only to an
account in **the owner's own name**. The Wire platform's AML agent checks the business
owner and the account against the citizen identity verification (DAN).

| Business type | Owner | Receiving account |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individual | The person verified with DAN | In that person's own name |
| Company | The director named in the registration confirms with DAN | In the company's name |

## Find your case

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Your own business | Register with your own DAN and connect an account in your name. |
| You build a site for a client | The client signs up and invites you as a member under **Team**. |
| An employee or relative is helping | The same: the owner signs up and invites them as a member. |
| One owner, several shops | Don't open a new business. Add a **project** per shop. |
| You also own a company with its own register | Register a separate business for that company. |
| Income should go to a company account | Choose **Registration type → Company** in the form; the account is in the company's name. If you already registered as an individual, register the company as a **new business**. The director confirms with DAN. |
| You already registered on someone's behalf | The real owner named in the registration confirms with DAN through Wire's link. |

<Callout type="warn">
  **One legal entity = one business.** The dashboard refuses a second business
  on the same register. Your next shop becomes a project in the business you
  already have.
</Callout>

## For developers and agencies

1. The client signs up at `app.wire.mn` **with their own DAN** and registers the business.
2. The client invites you as a member under **Team** in the dashboard.
3. You set up the projects, connections, API keys and webhooks in that business.
4. Income goes straight to the account in **the client's** name.

A member runs the business like the owner, but deletions are the owner's alone. Don't
register a client's business with your own DAN: you would become its owner, and it
would fail the account check.

## Several shops, several accounts

Create a separate project per shop inside one business (up to 10). Each project has its own API
key and webhook. To receive income into another account in your name, pass
`settlement_account` on each payment. See [Several accounts](/docs/guides/settlement-accounts).

## Not allowed

- Taking income through an account in the name of a relative, friend or employee.
- Collecting income on someone's behalf and passing it on later.
- Registering a business with another person's DAN.
- Opening a new business in place of a blocked one to keep taking payments.

## When the check fails

If the account is not in the business's name, new payments stop with
`403 account_holder_mismatch`. Completed transactions are not affected.

**Don't retry** this error. Add an account in the owner's name under **Accounts** in the
dashboard and payments resume automatically. If the registered owner does not match,
the real owner confirms with DAN.
