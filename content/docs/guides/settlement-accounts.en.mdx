---
title: Several accounts
description: When your business has several bank accounts, choose which one each payment goes to.
---

If your business has several bank accounts, you can choose which account
**each** payment settles into. A payment with no choice goes to your
**default** account (the one set per operator).

## Find your accounts

Accounts are added in the dashboard's **Accounts** section. List them with the
API:

```bash
curl https://api.wire.mn/v1/bank_accounts \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $WIRE_SECRET_KEY"
```

Each account comes with its `id` (`ba_…`), bank, the **last four digits** of
the account number, and the holder name. Only accounts with
`settlement_selectable: true` can be chosen for a payment.

<Callout type="info">
  An API key sees **only its own business's** accounts. Even when one owner has
  several businesses, one business's key cannot see — or choose — another
  business's account. All projects of a business share the same accounts.
</Callout>

## Choose an account per payment

Send `settlement_account` when you create the PaymentIntent:

```bash
curl https://api.wire.mn/v1/payment_intents \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $WIRE_SECRET_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: order-1024" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"amount": 15000, "settlement_account": "ba_m2qk7t4xw5nbv3rj6yhc2dpa4e"}'
```

- The payment goes **only to that account**. If the account was disconnected,
  the payment is refused — it never moves to another account.
- The intent is limited to operators that can name the account on each invoice
  (shown in `allowed_operators`).
- Errors: `400 settlement_account_invalid` (not your account, or not
  connected), `400 settlement_account_unsupported` (no such operator in
  `allowed_operators`).

## Without code — payment links

Pick a **Receiving account** when you create a
[payment link](/docs/guides/payment-links) and every payment through that link
goes to the chosen account.
