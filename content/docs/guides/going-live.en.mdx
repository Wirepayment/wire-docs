---
title: Activating an operator
description: Apply → review → electronic agreement → payments activate. The full path to accepting live payments.
---

To accept payments, you activate one of **the operators enabled for your
account**.
The flow has four steps:

<Callout type="info">
  Activation happens entirely in the dashboard — no code required. Once an
  operator is active and switched on for your project, nothing changes on the
  API side: PaymentIntents created without `allowed_operators` can use it
  automatically.
</Callout>

## 1. Apply

Pick an operator under **Connectors** in the dashboard and fill in the
step-by-step application:

- **Registration type** — individual or company.
- **Core details** — name, registration number, business activity. Individuals
  can fill these in one tap from their **verified citizen identity (DAN)**.
- **Address & contact** — location, phone, email. Your account email and
  verified phone are one tap away too.
- **Settlement account** — pick one of your linked bank accounts, or link a
  new one right in this step.

Review everything on the final step before submitting.

## 2. Review

The Wire team reviews applications during business hours. You are notified of
the decision **by email**, and the connector page tracks progress as staged
states (Details → Review → Active). If an application is declined you see the
reason, and the form comes back pre-filled so you only fix what was flagged.

## 3. Conclude the electronic agreement

Once your application is approved, you conclude the operator's **service
agreement electronically**:

1. Open and read the full agreement **as a PDF** (the confirmations unlock
   only after you have opened it).
2. Confirm you have read the fee schedule and the prohibited-services list.
3. Sign with your **verified citizen identity (DAN)** — legally equivalent to
   a written signature.

The agreement's merchant section carries the details you submitted in your
application; the signature block carries your verified name and registration
number. You can **view or download the signed PDF** from the connector page
at any time.

## 4. Payments activate

As soon as the agreement is concluded the connection activates automatically
and the operator becomes available in:

- the payment options on [Hosted checkout](/docs/guides/hosted-checkout),
- the payment page for [Payment links](/docs/guides/payment-links),
- the API — automatic routing, and `allowed_operators` if you narrow it.

Each **project** must also switch the operator on (project → Connectors).
Until it does, payments created with that project's API key cannot use the
operator, and the API answers `connector_required`.

## Pausing and resuming

Every active operator has a switch on the **Connectors** page. While switched
off, no new payments are created through that operator (it is removed from
automatic routing and the payment page); the connection and its configuration
stay intact and resume instantly when switched back on.

<Callout type="warn">
  Owner verification (phone + citizen identity) must be completed before an
  operator can be activated — see the
  [Quickstart](/docs/quickstart).
</Callout>
