---
title: Refund a charge
description: |
  Refunds a `succeeded` charge, fully (omit `amount`) or partially.
  The amount is reserved on the charge immediately (it shows in the
  charge's `amount_refunded`), so concurrent refunds can never exceed
  the charge.

  The resulting `status` depends on the operator:
  `succeeded` — refunded through the operator (a `charge.refunded`
  event is sent); `manual_required` — the operator does not support
  refunds through the API, so the refund is recorded and completed by
  Wire operations outside the API; `pending` — the operator accepted it
  but has not finished; `failed` — the operator refused and the
  reserved amount was released.

  **Errors** (besides the common ones):
  - `400 parameter_missing` (`charge`) — `charge` omitted.
  - `400 parameter_invalid` (`amount`) — `amount` is ≤ 0.
  - `404 resource_missing` — no such charge in this project.
  - `400 charge_not_refundable` (`charge`) — the charge is not
    `succeeded`, or is already fully refunded.
  - `400 amount_invalid` (`amount`) — more than the remaining
    refundable amount.
  - `400 reason_too_long` (`reason`) — over 500 bytes.
  - `424 refund_failed` — the operator could not process the refund.
