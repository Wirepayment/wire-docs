---
title: Connect an operator with your own credentials
description: |
  Stores credentials for an operator under your business. Credentials
  are encrypted at rest and never returned. A connection created here
  starts `inactive`; run `/test` to activate it. To take payments the
  operator must also be switched on for the project and have a
  settlement bank account (dashboard).

  Only `mode: "byo"` can be created here; connections Wire provisions
  for you come from the dashboard's connector application.

  **Errors:** `400 operator_unknown` (`operator`),
  `400 operator_connection_exists` (`operator`) — the business already
  has a connection for this operator,
  `400 operator_connection_invalid` (`mode`) — any mode other than
  `byo`,
  `400 operator_connection_invalid` (`credentials`) — empty
  `credentials` or an invalid `mode`.
