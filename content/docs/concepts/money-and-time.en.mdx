---
title: Money and time
description: How Wire represents amounts and timestamps.
---

## Money

All amounts are **whole Mongolian tögrög (MNT) as integers** — there is no
fractional part and no "cents".

- `15000` means **15,000 ₮**. `50000` means **50,000 ₮**.
- The currency is `MNT`.
- Never send decimals or floats; pass whole integers.
- A single PaymentIntent can be 1 to 1,000,000,000 ₮ (an operator may enforce
  its own minimum).

```json
{ "amount": 15000, "currency": "MNT" }
```

Display the number as-is with a thousands separator (`15,000 ₮`) — do **not**
divide by 100.

## Time

All timestamps are **Unix seconds in UTC** (e.g. `created`, `expires_at`).

- Store and compare timestamps in UTC.
- For display in Mongolia, convert to **Asia/Ulaanbaatar** and format as
  `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS`.

```text
created = 1717000000   →  2024-05-30 00:26:40 (Asia/Ulaanbaatar)
```
